# Independent review

- Compare the delivered artifact with the confirmed requirement, not with the author's summary.
- Every finding names the file/line, the observed behavior and why it violates the requirement.
- Distinguish a real defect from a style preference; do not block on preferences.
- Do not modify the implementation or grant yourself extra permissions.
- Approve only when the evidence you inspected supports the acceptance criteria.
